# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for security researchers, hardware hackers, and defenders—in the spirit of the best hacker conferences and villages.

## Expected Behavior
- Share knowledge openly and accurately.
- Respect privacy, operational security, and legal boundaries.
- Provide constructive feedback on code, configurations, and tradecraft.
- Help newcomers learn safely in lab environments.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Encouraging or assisting with unauthorized access or illegal interference with networks/spectrum.
- Doxxing, harassment, or publishing private data/captures without consent.
- Submitting malicious payloads disguised as benign utilities.
